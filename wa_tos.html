📌 1. Purpose of These Groups

These WhatsApp groups are informal, student-run spaces created to help ASPU students share study materials, past exams, test banks, and academic support with one another by cohort year.

هذه المجموعات هي مساحات طلابية غير رسمية تهدف إلى مساعدة طلاب الجامعة على تبادل المواد الدراسية والامتحانات السابقة والدعم الأكاديمي حسب سنة الدفعة.

👤 2. Role of the Admin

The admin(s) of these groups volunteer their time to organize and moderate the community. Admins do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of any material shared by members, and are not responsible for advice, opinions, or content posted by other participants.

🔒 3. Admin Privacy & No-Harassment Policy

  • Members may not harass, threaten, defame, dox, or attack the admin(s) — publicly or privately — regarding decisions made about group management, content removal, or member removal.
  • Disagreements with an admin decision should be raised respectfully and privately. Abusive behavior toward an admin is grounds for immediate removal and may be reported to WhatsApp or relevant university authorities.
  • Admins reserve the right to keep their personal information (phone number visibility, identity, contact details) private and are not obligated to disclose it beyond what WhatsApp itself displays.

✅ 4. Acceptable Use

  • Share academic resources relevant to the cohort/group (notes, past exams, study guides).
  • Ask and answer academic questions respectfully.
  • Keep discussions relevant to university/academic matters.

🚫 5. Prohibited Conduct

  • Spam, advertising, unrelated promotions, or repeated off-topic messages.
  • Hate speech, harassment, bullying, or discriminatory remarks toward any member.
  • Sharing malicious links, viruses, or phishing content.
  • Impersonating university staff, the admin, or other students.
  • Sharing another member's personal information without consent.
  • Any illegal activity, including academic dishonesty in violation of university policy.
