Step by step

خطوة بخطوة
  1. Find the search boxIt is at the top of the home page, and in the Search tab of the app.
  2. Type the subject or its codeA few letters are enough: "calc", "MATH101" or a doctor's name. Arabic names work too.
  3. Press EnterResults load right away: subject pages first, then files found in the Google Drive. No waiting or reloading needed.
  4. Narrow it downUse the filters to show only subject pages, only Drive files, or one year.
  5. Open and saveFolders and PDFs open in place. Save the ones you need so they are one tap away next time.

Tips

نصائح
  • Search the course name, not the file name. Files are named by the students who uploaded them.
  • Nothing found? Try the English and the Arabic name.
  • Open the subject page to see every year and doctor in one place.
  • New files show up in Latest uploads.
