About UniHub

A free, student-run study library and campus guide for university students in Jordan.

What UniHub is

ما هو UniHub

UniHub (formerly ASU Bank) started as a shared folder of summaries that one student passed to friends. It grew into a library of study materials sorted by college and subject: summaries, lecture notes, past papers, test banks, presentations and guides. Most of it comes from students of Applied Science Private University, the University of Jordan and Yarmouk University, who share what helped them so the next class has an easier start.

Next to the library, UniHub collects the practical things students look for every week: the academic calendar with a live countdown, bus times, the Ramadan lecture times, how hall numbers work, the grading scale, placement test preparation and answers to common questions.

Who runs it

من يديره

UniHub is run by students, for students. It is not made by or affiliated with any university, and nothing here is an official announcement. Dates and times are copied from what the universities publish, but they can change, so always check official sources for anything final.

The website is free to use. Some guide pages show ads from Google, which help pay for keeping the site and the free iPhone and Android apps running. Pages with study files, forms, accounts and chat have no ads, and the apps have none. See the privacy policy for how ads and data are handled.

How files get here

كيف تُضاف الملفات
  • Students upload files through the upload pages or the apps.
  • Each file is checked, given a clear title in English and Arabic, and filed under the right college and subject.
  • Names and student ID numbers are hidden on exam papers before they are published.
  • Anyone who owns the rights to a file can ask for it to be removed, and it is taken down. See the copyright page.
