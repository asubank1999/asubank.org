Before you upload

  • PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, images or ZIP · up to 25 MB per file.
  • Hide your university number and name on scans.
  • No illegal, private or copyrighted material. Files that break the rules are removed.
  • Use a clear name, e.g. “Calculus 1 Midterm 2025 Dr Ahmad.pdf”.
  • New files appear in Latest uploads and in Search.
