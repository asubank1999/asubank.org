**New in 1.9.0**
- Friends: add classmates, accept requests, poke them or ask to meet, study or grab coffee, and share posts in a new Friends tab

**New in 1.8.0**
- **Report anything**: press and hold a chat message, open a profile, or use the menu on any file to report it. Anyone can report a problem from More, even without an account.
- **Block people**: you won't see their messages or get notifications from them. Undo it any time from your account.
- **Community rules and a privacy policy**, both in More.
- **Safer chat**: abusive words are filtered, and deleting your account removes your name from your old messages.

**New in 1.7.0**
- **Search help**: type a course in Arabic or English, even with mistakes. The app translates it, suggests the right word and subject, and searches both languages.
- **Major and college** on your profile, set in Edit profile and shown to other students.
- **More badges**, such as Mentor, Top student, Class rep, Translator and Legend.
- **Arabic everywhere**: the student guide, FAQs, shortcuts, halls, rules and every other page now switch to Arabic, not only the buttons. Material names stay as they were uploaded.
- **Tabs fixed**: tap a tab you are already in, like More, to go back to its first page.

**New in 1.6.0**
- **Profiles**: add a profile photo, a line about yourself and your phone number (shown only if you choose). Tap anyone in the chat to see their profile, call them or open WhatsApp.
- **Who's online**: see who is online right now and when others were last seen, with a green dot on their photo.
- **Edit your messages** after sending them (they show "edited").
- **Choose your notifications**: chat messages, replies to you, announcements and new uploads, each on or off, and mute the rooms you don't want.
- **Special badges** such as Helper, Support, Donor, Tutor and Verified, given by the team, shown on profiles and in the chat.
- **Language**: English, Arabic, Spanish or your phone's language, in More > Language.
- **Rate our service** and the **Discord voting** are now native screens, and they count together with the website.
- For admins: add, rename, reorder, lock, archive, clear and delete chat rooms, pin a message to the top of a room, and give or remove badges.
- **Notifications fixed**: the app now checks every 15 minutes in the background and right away when you open it, so you get new messages, replies, announcements and uploads.
- **ASU shortcuts** (my.asu.edu.jo, e-learning, Outlook and more) now open in your browser so they always load.

**New in 1.5.0**
- **Accounts**: sign in or create an account with just a username and password (university email and ID are optional). The same account works in the iPhone app, the Android app and on asubank.org.
- **Student chat**: real-time rooms (General, Study help, Materials and more) where signed-in students help each other. Reply to messages, copy them, and delete your own.
- **Notifications** for new messages ("New message from …") and team announcements. Pick every message, announcements only, or off.
- **Themes**: match your phone, Light, Dark, or Pitch black for OLED screens, plus 7 accent colors.
- **Student submissions, now native**: browse the board, search it and submit your presentation from the app.
- The ASU Bank logo greets you for a moment when the app opens. Upload is now a sheet you can open from anywhere.
- When a new version is out, the app tells you as soon as you open it.
- Enhanced **Thanks list** with stats and a founder card.
- For admins: an **Admin tab** to see every registered student, ban or unban, reset passwords, make admins, delete accounts, delete chat messages and uploads, send announcements and see the activity log.

**Install:** download `ASUBank-1.4.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. Android asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or files app the first time. Android 8.0 or newer.

The app tells you when a new version is out.

Free project by students. Not an official Applied Science University app.
