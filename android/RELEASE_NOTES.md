**ASU Bank for Android** — the native app for [asubank.org](https://asubank.org).

- Search every material, plus the shared Google Drive, from one search box
- PDFs, slides and documents open natively in the app (pinch to zoom), Drive folders open as native lists
- Save materials, see what you opened recently, upload materials to share with other students
- Calendar, schedules, FAQs and every asubank.org page as native screens
- Light and dark mode

**New in 1.4.0**
- **Course matcher, now native**: courses, topics and free times on a simple grid, matched with classmates by email
- **Request a material, now native**: pick a service, attach your PDF, pay by CliQ (alias ALANQ99) or in person
- **Your uploads show up in Latest uploads** right away
- **Grading scale** redesigned, with term and cumulative average calculators
- **How to search** guide rewritten for the app
- **WhatsApp groups & resources** grouped by college and year, searchable, one tap to join
- New **Thanks list**, **Student ads** and **Support ASU Bank** screens

**New in 1.3.0**
- Rate your teachers, fully native: search, sort, averages out of 10, and anonymous ratings in a few taps
- University halls finder: type a hall number (e.g. 1206) to see the building, floor and room
- Smoother: screens slide and fade, long lists scroll smoothly, and the app is pre-optimized on install
- Fixed Google Drive folders that looked empty: every file inside a folder now shows up
- Search no longer shows the same file several times

**Install:** download `ASUBank-1.4.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. Android asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or files app the first time. Android 8.0 or newer.

The app tells you when a new version is out.

Volunteer, non-profit project by students. Not an official Applied Science University app.
