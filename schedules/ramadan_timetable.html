How to find your Ramadan lecture time

During Ramadan, lectures are shorter and start later. You don't need a new timetable: find your lecture's normal start time in the "Regular Semester" column, then read across to the "Ramadan Time" column on the same row. The slot number stays the same.

For example, a lecture that is normally on Sunday and Tuesday from 09:30 to 11:00 is slot 2, so in Ramadan it runs from 10:15 to 11:15. A lecture normally at 14:00 (slot 5) becomes 14:00 to 15:00.

Tips for the month

  • Breaks between lectures are only 15 minutes, so plan your route between buildings in advance.
  • Bus times may change during Ramadan too; check the bus timetable before your first day.
  • Late lectures end around 16:15, which leaves time to get home before iftar on most routes.
  • Ask your instructor if quizzes or office hours move; they don't always follow the table.
