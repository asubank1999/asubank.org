Privacy policy

What ASU Bank collects, why, and how to delete it.

ما الذي يجمعه بنك ASU ولماذا وكيف تحذفه.

Who we are

ASU Bank is a free, volunteer, student-run project. It is not made by or owned by Applied Science Private University. Questions go to necro144@yahoo.com.

What we collect when you make an account

Username, display name and a scrambled copy of your password.

Only if you add them: email, university ID, phone number, profile photo, bio, major, college, year and interests.

Your chat messages, posts and likes, your friends and friend requests, pokes you send and get, who you blocked, when you were last active, the reports you send and the group links you ask us to add.

What other students can see

Your name, username, photo, bio, major, college, year, interests, badges, posts, how many friends you have and when you were last online. Your phone number shows only if you turn that on. Your email and university ID are visible only to you and the admins.

When you share a post or story with everyone, anyone can see it on the website and in the apps, also without an account, along with your name, photo and badges. Posts and stories shared with ASU students or friends only, and the rest of your profile, stay for signed-in people.

Private messages and their files are seen only by you and the person you write to. They are stored on our Google account and are not end-to-end encrypted. They can be looked at when someone reports a message.

What you send us without an account

Files you upload, teacher ratings, course matcher forms and material requests go to our Google Drive and Sheets, with the details you type in. Don’t put your name or student ID in files you upload.

Files you submit for the campus screen go to a separate folder in our Google Drive, and anyone with the link can open them. Photoshoot bookings send your name, phone, email and session details to the photographer.

Search text the app can’t translate by itself goes to Google Translate through our server, without your account.

Where it is kept and who gets it

Your data is stored on Google services (Sheets, Drive and Apps Script) in the project owner’s account. Only admins can open it. We don’t sell it or use it for advertising.

The Home screen’s weather comes from Open-Meteo, which is asked about the campus only and never about you or your location. On the website your browser makes this request itself, so Open-Meteo sees your internet address, as any website you load does. Your lectures, tab bar and sounds stay on your phone.

The apps have no analytics. The website uses Google Analytics to count visits.

Ads on the website

Some pages of the website may show ads from Google AdSense. Ad money goes to the person who runs ASU Bank. The apps have no ads.

Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads in Google’s Ads Settings, and read how Google uses data on sites that use its services.

We don’t give advertisers your account details, messages or files.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time. See the page on deleting your account. Your profile, photo, friends, pokes and posts are removed, and your old messages stay in the chat as “Deleted user”.

To remove a file or anything else, report it or email us.

Children

ASU Bank is for university students and isn’t meant for children under 13.
