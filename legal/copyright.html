Copyright and removal requests

If you own something on UniHub, or represent the owner, you can ask us to remove it.

إذا كنت تملك محتوى على UniHub أو تمثل مالكه، يمكنك طلب إزالته.

What UniHub is

UniHub is a free project run by a student. Most files are shared by students or link to files they host on their own Drive or OneDrive. UniHub doesn't claim to own them, and it doesn't sell them.

How to ask

Send a report and choose Copyright, or email necro144@yahoo.com. Please include:

  1. A link to the file, or its exact name and subject.
  2. What you own, for example the book, exam or course material.
  3. Your name and how to reach you, and your role if you act for someone else.
  4. A statement that the request is true to the best of your knowledge.

What happens next

We aim to answer within 7 days, and we take a file down while we look into a clear request. When we remove something we hide it from every part of UniHub. A file hosted on someone else's account can only be deleted by that person or by their storage provider, so we may point you to them too.

If your file was removed and you believe that was a mistake, tell us the same way and explain why.

Accounts that keep sharing other people's work without permission can be closed.

طلب الإزالة

أرسل بلاغاً واختر حقوق النشر، أو راسلنا على البريد أعلاه، واذكر رابط الملف أو اسمه، وما تملكه، واسمك وطريقة التواصل معك، وتأكيداً بأن طلبك صحيح حسب علمك. نحاول الرد خلال 7 أيام، ونخفي الملف أثناء مراجعة الطلب الواضح.
