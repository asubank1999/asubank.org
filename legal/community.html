The rules

Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, threats or hate speech.

No sexual or violent content, and nothing illegal.

Don’t share other people’s private information.

Only share files you have the right to share.

No spam or advertising.

Zero tolerance

We have zero tolerance for abuse. Admins can remove content and ban accounts that break these rules, without warning.

Report and block

Press and hold a message, open a profile, or use the menu on a file to report it. You can also block anyone from their profile or a message. Reports go straight to the admins.

Copyright

If you own something on ASU Bank or represent the owner and want it removed, report the file or email necro144@yahoo.com with a link to it.
