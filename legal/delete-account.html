Delete your account

How to delete your UniHub account and data.

كيف تحذف حسابك وبياناتك في بنك ASU.

In the app

Open the Chat tab or More and go to your account.

Choose Delete my account and enter your password.

On the website

Sign in at asubank.org/account, then choose to delete your account and enter your password.

Can’t sign in?

Email necro144@yahoo.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

What happens

Your profile, photo, email, university ID, phone number and sign-ins are deleted straight away. Your old messages stay in the chat as “Deleted user”, with nothing that points to you.
