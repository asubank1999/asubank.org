## ASU Bank for iPhone

Summaries, past papers and test banks for Applied Science Private University students, in a native iOS app.
ASU Bank is a free, volunteer, non-profit project. It is not made by or affiliated with the university.

### What's new in 1.6.0
- **Profiles**: add a profile photo, a line about yourself and your phone number (shown only if you choose). Tap anyone in the chat to see their profile, call them or open WhatsApp.
- **Who's online**: see who is online right now and when others were last seen, with a green dot on their photo.
- **Edit your messages** after sending them (they show "edited").
- **Choose your notifications**: chat messages, replies to you, announcements and new uploads, each on or off, and mute the rooms you don't want.
- **Special badges** such as Helper, Support, Donor, Tutor and Verified, given by the team, shown on profiles and in the chat.
- **Language**: English, Arabic, Spanish or your phone's language, in More > Language.
- **Rate our service** and the **Discord voting** are now native screens, and they count together with the website.
- For admins: add, rename, reorder, lock, archive, clear and delete chat rooms, pin a message to the top of a room, and give or remove badges.
- Notifications now also cover replies to you and new uploads. On iPhone they arrive when iOS lets the app check in the background.

### What's new in 1.5.0
- **Accounts**: sign in or create an account with just a username and password (university email and ID are optional). The same account works in the iPhone app, the Android app and on asubank.org.
- **Student chat**: real-time rooms (General, Study help, Materials and more) where signed-in students help each other. Reply to messages, copy them, and delete your own.
- **Notifications** for new messages ("New message from …") and team announcements. Pick every message, announcements only, or off.
- **Themes**: match your phone, Light, Dark, or Pitch black for OLED screens, plus 7 accent colors.
- **Student submissions, now native**: browse the board, search it and submit your presentation from the app.
- The ASU Bank logo greets you for a moment when the app opens. Upload is now a sheet you can open from anywhere.
- When a new version is out, the app tells you as soon as you open it.
- Enhanced **Thanks list** with stats and a founder card.
- For admins: an **Admin tab** to see every registered student, ban or unban, reset passwords, make admins, delete accounts, delete chat messages and uploads, send announcements and see the activity log.

### What's new in 1.4.0
- **Course matcher, now native**: fill in your courses, topics and free times on a simple grid, and get matched with classmates by email.
- **Request a material, now native**: pick a service, attach your PDF, choose CliQ (alias ALANQ99) or in-person payment, and send it from the app.
- **Your uploads show up in Latest uploads** right away, together with the newest files in the shared upload folder.
- **Grading scale** redesigned, with a term average and cumulative average calculator.
- **How to search** guide rewritten for the app: try a search right from the guide.
- **WhatsApp groups & resources**: grouped by college and year, searchable, one tap to join.
- New **Thanks list**, **Student ads** and **Support ASU Bank** screens. CliQ now uses the alias ALANQ99 (Bank of Jordan).
- Teacher ratings connect more reliably.

### What's new in 1.3.0
- **Rate your teachers**, now fully native: search every rated teacher, sort by top rated or most votes, see averages out of 10 with color bars, and rate anyone in a few taps. Still 100% anonymous.
- **University halls finder**: type the number on a hall's door (e.g. 1206) and see the building, floor and room instantly, in English and Arabic.
- Works on **iOS 15** and newer, so it now runs on every iPhone from the iPhone 6s up.
- **Fixed Google Drive folders that looked empty**: every file inside a folder now shows up.
- Search no longer shows the same file several times.
- asubank.org links shared with you open straight in the app.

### What's new in 1.2.0
- Every material opens natively: PDFs in a built-in PDF reader, slides, documents and sheets in Quick Look, and Google Drive folders as native lists you can browse and search.
- Fixed files that showed "No preview available" (Google Drive shortcuts now open the real file or folder).
- Dead or private links are hidden. If you open one from Saved, you can request it instead.
- Opened files are kept offline; More > Storage shows the size and clears the cache.
