## ASU Bank for iPhone

Summaries, past papers and test banks for Applied Science Private University students, in a native iOS app.
ASU Bank is a free, volunteer, non-profit project. It is not made by or affiliated with the university.

### What's new in 1.4.0
- **Course matcher, now native**: fill in your courses, topics and free times on a simple grid, and get matched with classmates by email.
- **Request a material, now native**: pick a service, attach your PDF, choose CliQ (alias ALANQ99) or in-person payment, and send it from the app.
- **Your uploads show up in Latest uploads** right away, together with the newest files in the shared upload folder.
- **Grading scale** redesigned, with a term average and cumulative average calculator.
- **How to search** guide rewritten for the app: try a search right from the guide.
- **WhatsApp groups & resources**: grouped by college and year, searchable, one tap to join.
- New **Thanks list**, **Student ads** and **Support ASU Bank** screens. CliQ now uses the alias ALANQ99 (Bank of Jordan).
- Teacher ratings connect more reliably.

### What's new in 1.3.0
- **Rate your teachers**, now fully native: search every rated teacher, sort by top rated or most votes, see averages out of 10 with color bars, and rate anyone in a few taps. Still 100% anonymous.
- **University halls finder**: type the number on a hall's door (e.g. 1206) and see the building, floor and room instantly, in English and Arabic.
- Works on **iOS 15** and newer, so it now runs on every iPhone from the iPhone 6s up.
- **Fixed Google Drive folders that looked empty**: every file inside a folder now shows up.
- Search no longer shows the same file several times.
- asubank.org links shared with you open straight in the app.

### What's new in 1.2.0
- Every material opens natively: PDFs in a built-in PDF reader, slides, documents and sheets in Quick Look, and Google Drive folders as native lists you can browse and search.
- Fixed files that showed "No preview available" (Google Drive shortcuts now open the real file or folder).
- Dead or private links are hidden. If you open one from Saved, you can request it instead.
- Opened files are kept offline; More > Storage shows the size and clears the cache.

### Install (sideloading)
